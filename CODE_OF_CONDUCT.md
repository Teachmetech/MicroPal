# Code of Conduct

MicroPal welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat others with respect, give constructive feedback, assume good intent where reasonable, and take responsibility when your words or actions cause harm.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information without consent are not acceptable. Disagreement about technical work is welcome when it remains focused on the work and considerate of the people involved.

These expectations apply to repository issues, reviews, discussions, project events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner using an existing contact method on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/Teachmetech). If no other private route is available, use [a private advisory](https://github.com/Teachmetech/MicroPal/security/advisories/new) and identify the report as a conduct matter. Do not post private evidence or personal information in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports with care, limit disclosure to what is needed to respond, and consider the people affected. They may remove content, request a correction, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a participant according to the seriousness and repetition of the behavior. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is itself a violation.

If a report involves a maintainer, contact another maintainer where available. Do not use a reporting route controlled only by the person involved when doing so would put you at risk.
